DOCUMIND.AI — DEMO VIDEO SCRIPT
Duration: approximately 3 to 4 minutes
Tone: clear, confident, professional

---

OPENING (0:00 to 0:30)

[Show the DocuMind.ai homepage]

"Every enterprise is drowning in documents. Contracts, reports, 
research papers, compliance filings — critical information locked 
away in hundreds of PDFs that nobody has time to read.

DocuMind.ai changes that. Upload any document, ask any question, 
and get a verified answer with the exact source cited — in seconds.

Let me show you how it works."

---

SECTION 1 — UPLOAD (0:30 to 1:00)

[Drag and drop a contract PDF onto the upload zone]

"I have a service contract here. I'll drag it into DocuMind.ai.

The system is now parsing the document, detecting its structure — 
headings, paragraphs, table cells — and indexing every passage 
for both keyword and semantic search.

[Status changes to completed in the sidebar]

It's ready. Three pages, fully indexed."

---

SECTION 2 — BASIC QUERY (1:00 to 1:45)

[Type in the chat: "what are the payment terms?"]

"Now I'll ask the most common question a finance team would have 
about a contract — what are the payment terms?

[Answer appears]

The system found the answer on page one: net payment is due within 
30 days of invoice date, with a 1.5 percent monthly late fee.

Notice the green badge next to that claim — that means the NLI 
verifier confirmed this sentence is directly supported by the 
source text. No guessing, no hallucination.

[Click the citation]

I'll click the citation. The document opens on the right, and 
the exact passage is highlighted in yellow. This is where that 
answer came from."

---

SECTION 3 — HARDER QUERY (1:45 to 2:30)

[Type in the chat: "what happens if either party wants to terminate?"]

"Now a harder question — what are the termination conditions?

[Answer appears with amber and green badges]

The system found the termination clause on page three. 
Either party can terminate with 30 days written notice, 
and all outstanding invoices become immediately payable.

Two claims here — both verified. The green badge means 
high confidence. If a claim were amber or red it would 
signal lower confidence so the user knows to double-check.

This is the Critic Agent working behind the scenes. 
If the first search does not produce enough evidence, 
it automatically refines the query and searches again 
before generating the answer."

---

SECTION 4 — WHAT MAKES IT DIFFERENT (2:30 to 3:00)

[Show the system architecture diagram briefly]

"Under the hood DocuMind.ai is doing several things that 
standard RAG systems do not do.

It combines vector search with keyword search and uses 
Reciprocal Rank Fusion to get the best of both. 
Exact terms like clause numbers and dollar amounts — 
things that confuse pure semantic search — are found reliably.

Before any document is indexed, sensitive data is automatically 
redacted — names, social security numbers, financial identifiers — 
so personal information can never appear in an answer.

And every claim is checked by a DeBERTa NLI model before 
it reaches the user. If more than 20 percent of claims 
fail verification, the Critic Agent retries with a 
better query. The system knows when it does not know."

---

SECTION 5 — RESULTS (3:00 to 3:30)

[Show the evaluation results slide or table]

"We evaluated DocuMind.ai against standard RAG on a benchmark 
of 120 enterprise documents across four categories — contracts, 
financial reports, research papers, and scanned forms.

Faithfulness — meaning no hallucinations — scored 0.91 
compared to 0.77 for standard RAG. That is a 14 point improvement.

Answer relevance scored 0.88. Average query time is under 
2 seconds for retrieval and under 10 seconds end to end 
including verification.

The ablation study confirms every component contributes — 
removing any single piece drops performance."

Thank you."
